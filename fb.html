Crispy Shrimp Fritters

  • 1 lb peeled, chopped shrimp
  • Corn, scallions and fresh parsley
  • A light flour-and-egg batter
  • Lemon and dipping sauce to serve
30 min total

15 minutes to prep, 15 minutes to cook. Serves four as an appetizer or light meal.

Kitchen note: cook shrimp all the way through.

The centers should be opaque and firm, and the fritters should reach 145°F / 63°C. This recipe contains shellfish, egg and wheat. Always check ingredient labels and adapt the recipe for any food allergies at your table.

The recipe

Simple ingredients, seriously crisp results.

Start with 1 lb (450 g) raw shrimp, peeled, deveined and roughly chopped. Add 1/2 cup corn, 3 sliced scallions, 2 tablespoons chopped parsley and 1 minced garlic clove.

For the batter, use 1/2 cup all-purpose flour, 1 teaspoon baking powder, 1 egg and 1/4 cup cold water. Season with 1 teaspoon paprika, 1/2 teaspoon salt, black pepper and a little lemon zest.

Fold everything together just until combined. Fry small spoonfuls in a thin layer of neutral oil until deeply golden, about 2 to 3 minutes per side. Drain briefly and serve while hot.

Chef's tip

Pat the shrimp dry before mixing and keep the batter cold. Less moisture means crisper fritters, and a cold batter browns beautifully in the hot oil.

Use fresh, dry shrimp

Roughly chopped shrimp gives each fritter a juicy, satisfying bite. Dry it well so the batter stays light instead of watery.

Fold the batter gently

Stir only until no dry flour remains. Overmixing develops gluten and can make the fritters dense instead of tender.

Keep the oil hot

Fry at about 350°F / 175°C. The fritters should sizzle immediately without darkening before the shrimp is cooked through.

How to make them

Four easy steps to golden fritters.

Mix lightly, fry in small batches and serve right away for the best contrast between crisp edges and tender centers.

Step 01

Prep the shrimp

Pat the peeled shrimp dry and chop into small pieces. Slice the scallions, mince the garlic and chop the fresh parsley.

Step 02

Mix the batter

Whisk flour, baking powder and seasonings. Add the egg and cold water, then fold in shrimp, corn, herbs and garlic.

Step 03

Fry until golden

Spoon small portions into 350°F / 175°C oil. Flatten gently and cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side in small batches.

Step 04

Drain and serve

Transfer to a rack or paper towel, add a pinch of flaky salt and serve hot with lemon wedges and your favorite dipping sauce.

For a quick sauce, stir together 1/2 cup mayonnaise, 1 tablespoon lemon juice, 1 teaspoon hot sauce and a pinch of salt.
